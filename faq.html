Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about courses, online learning and admissions.

Are classes conducted online?

Yes. The current academy model is online learning.

Who can enrol?

Children and adults can enquire about suitable courses. Course placement is discussed with the academy.

How do I book a free trial?

Complete the free trial request form. The academy will contact you to confirm availability.

Which courses are available?

The course catalogue includes Noorani Qaida, Quran Reading, Tajweed, Hifz, Recitation, Arabic Language, Islamic Studies, Daily Duas and Salah, and Quran Translation and Tafseer.

Which currencies can I view prices in?

The pricing page supports indicative display in INR, USD and QAR. Live payment processing is not yet connected.

Is the Qatar branch open?

No. Qatar is a planned future branch location and is marked Coming Soon.

How do online classes get scheduled?

Please submit your preferred timing and time zone. The academy will discuss available schedules.
